# Release notes: demo-v1 (draft)

Nadjet V1 for the live demo. The tag is created by the maintainer on `main` after the
`dev` to `main` promotion and a green CI (not yet done).

## What it does

- Reads existing CCTV (simulated by looping real clips) over RTSP, tracks vehicles and people, fires
  rules and an AI sweep, and has Qwen3-VL on NVIDIA Brev check each candidate. The AI never confirms
  a collision alone; every AI value carries its source.
- Creates incidents with evidence (signed, tamper-evident), priority P1 to P4 with reasons, merge and
  lifecycle, operator confirmation and rejection with reasons, manual incidents.
- Recommends the nearest suitable unit and hospital by road ETA, dispatches by SMS and web push, and
  reads SMS replies (`OK`, status codes, compact triage).
- Responder PWA, hospital pre-alert view (READY or DIVERT), citizen one-tap report with GPS-SMS fallback.
- "Try it yourself": upload a clip or use a phone as a live camera, in an isolated sandbox.
- KPIs, evaluation page on held-out clips, camera health, admin, retention.
- Arabic (RTL), French and English.
- Production deployment on a VPS with authenticated video streams and GPU workers on Brev
  (outbound only): `docs/DEPLOYMENT.md`.

## New in this issue (#22)

- `nadjet-api seed-demo` (`make seed-demo`): idempotent users, units, facilities and cameras; never incidents.
- `docs/demo/RUNBOOK.md`, `docs/demo/RESULTS.md`, and `docs/traceability.md` (generated by
  `scripts/traceability.py`, reported in CI).

## Known limits (state them on stage)

- Research footage, not Algerian CCTV: results will differ in the field.
- Misses: small, distant, night-time or occluded crashes. False triggers: stop-and-go traffic.
- Facilities, units and capabilities are simulated and labelled so.
- Integration-ready (RTSP/ONVIF, CAP 1.2, SMS), not integrated with any government system.
- Annotated video re-encoding uses x264 (GPL) through PyAV/FFmpeg: a licence decision is open for any
  distribution beyond the demo (PRD D-06).
- Success-criteria measurements on production are pending: see `docs/demo/RESULTS.md`.
